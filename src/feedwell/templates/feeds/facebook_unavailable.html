Connect Facebook

Reading your personal Facebook News Feed isn't possible through Facebook's API anymore, for any app. Meta removed that permission for third-party apps in 2018 and it has never come back — this isn't a feedwell limitation or something an App Review can unlock.

What's still technically possible is reading posts from Facebook Pages you manage (not your personal feed), which requires Business Verification and App Review with Meta. That's not wired up yet. If you manage a Page and want that instead, let us know and we'll build it.
